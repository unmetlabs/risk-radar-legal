Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what RiskRadar, run by Muhammad Usman Jamil, Lahore, Pakistan, collects about sellers and their customers, why, and for how long. We don’t sell personal data and don’t show ads.

Customer mobile numbers are turned into a one-way code (a keyed hash) as soon as they reach our servers. We keep the code, not the number, so we can count parcels per number without storing who the number belongs to.

Sellers and their staff

  • Mobile number: stored encrypted and as a one-way code, used to log in with a code by SMS.
  • Shop name, city and an optional page link; the devices you log in from (name, type, last used); when you accepted the terms.
  • Business verification: CNIC or NTN images and the number our reviewer types in.
  • Courier API keys: encrypted; only the last 4 characters are shown back to you.
  • Payments: plan or pack, amount, method, receipt images and payment references.
  • Checks you make: the one-way code of the number, how you checked (app, WhatsApp, share) and the result. Your check history in the app is saved only on your phone, encrypted, and cleared when you log out.
  • Extra WhatsApp numbers you add for the bot: stored encrypted and as a one-way code.

Customers (buyers)

From couriers and uploaded files we keep, per parcel: the tracking number, the one-way code of the customer’s mobile number, city, booking date and delivery status. We do not collect customers’ names or addresses.

If a customer uses our correction page, we also keep the reason and note they write. For a correction request we keep an encrypted copy of their number only to send the decision by SMS, and delete it once sent. Their IP address is checked by Cloudflare’s robot check and, for rate limits, kept only as a one-way code.

What shops can see about a customer

A masked number (like 0300 ••• 4567), delivered and returned counts for the last 12 months, how many shops shipped to it, and a label calculated only from those counts. Never which shops, never names or addresses.

Why we use it

  • To give check results and keep the shared counts correct.
  • To keep accounts secure: login codes, spotting misuse (unusual checking patterns pause checks automatically) and preventing duplicate accounts.
  • To verify businesses, take payments and answer support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run parts of RiskRadar for us, under their own security terms:

  • Hosting and storage: Railway, Neon, Upstash, Cloudflare (R2) and Vercel.
  • Messages: Meta (WhatsApp replies from our bot) and our SMS provider (login codes and decisions).
  • Payments: Safepay and banks, for the payments you make.
  • Robot check: Cloudflare Turnstile on the correction page.
  • Error reports: Sentry receives crash details without personal data.
  • Couriers: we read your shipments from PostEx or other couriers with the key you give us.

We share data with authorities only when Pakistani law requires it. Our providers’ servers are outside Pakistan (for example in India, Singapore, the EU or the US); data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

How long we keep it

  • Delivery outcomes and check records: 12 months (shipments 13 months, so the oldest month of your analytics is complete).
  • Verification images and the typed-in CNIC/NTN number: 30 days after our decision.
  • Correction requests: 12 months after the decision; the customer’s number is deleted once the decision is sent.
  • Payment receipts: 12 months after the payment is settled. Invoice amounts stay while the account exists.
  • Login code requests: 12 months. Sessions: deleted 30 days after they expire.
  • Waitlist sign-ups: 12 months.
  • Closed accounts: everything is deleted 30 days after closing, including files, and the shared counts are recalculated without them.
  • Our admin audit log is kept to show who did what; it holds no customer numbers.

How we protect it

Encryption (AES-256) for numbers, keys and documents; one-way codes for customer numbers; private file storage with short-lived links; screenshots blocked in the app; two-step login and an audit log for our staff; and regular security checks.

Your choices and rights

  • Sellers can see and edit their details, disconnect couriers, remove staff and bot numbers, and close the account in the app.
  • Customers can see the counts linked to their number and ask for a correction on our website, or by emailing usmanjamil047@gmail.com with the number. We reply within 14 days.
  • For access, correction or deletion requests beyond this, write to usmanjamil047@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, as far as the law allows.

Children, changes and contact

RiskRadar is for businesses and not meant for anyone under 18.

If this policy changes, the app asks you to accept the new version. Contact: usmanjamil047@gmail.com, Lahore, Pakistan.

Delete your RiskRadar account

Last updated: 1 October 2026

RiskRadar is published on Google Play by unmetlabs. This page explains how to delete your RiskRadar account and what happens to your data.

Account owners: delete it in the app

  1. Open RiskRadar and log in.
  2. Tap More.
  3. Tap Close account.
  4. Type CLOSE and confirm.

Everyone on the account is logged out at once and your courier keys are forgotten straight away. Everything else is deleted 30 days later. This can’t be undone.

Staff members, or if you can’t use the app

Email usmanjamil047@gmail.com from any address and include the mobile number you log in with. We confirm the number by SMS code, then delete the account within 30 days. Staff members can also ask the account owner to remove them from the team.

What is deleted

  • Login numbers, shop name, city and page link.
  • Logged-in devices and sessions.
  • Business verification images and document numbers.
  • Courier connections and the shipments imported from them.
  • Payments, invoices and receipt images.
  • Records of the checks you made and the extra WhatsApp numbers you added.
  • Your parcels are removed from the shared counts other shops see.
  • Check history saved on your phone is deleted when you log out.

What is kept

  • Our admin audit log, which records what our staff did (for example approving a verification). It holds no customer numbers and is kept for security.
  • Records your bank or wallet keeps of payments you made are outside RiskRadar.

Delete only some data

  • A courier key: More → Couriers → the courier → Disconnect. The key is deleted at once; imported shipments are kept until they are 13 months old or your account is deleted.
  • An extra WhatsApp number: More → WhatsApp bot → Remove.
  • Anything else: email usmanjamil047@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Terms of use

Last updated: 1 October 2026

RiskRadar helps online sellers in Pakistan see a customer’s delivery history across shops before shipping a cash-on-delivery order. It is run by Muhammad Usman Jamil, Lahore, Pakistan (“we”).

By creating an account or tapping “Agree and continue” in the app, you accept these terms and our privacy policy. If you accept for a business, you confirm you may act for it.

Who can use RiskRadar

You must be at least 18 and run or work for a business that sells to customers in Pakistan. Give true details about your business and keep them up to date.

The account owner is responsible for staff they add and for everything done from the account.

What a check shows

A check shows, for one mobile number and the last 12 months: how many parcels were delivered, how many were returned, how many shops shipped to it, and a label (such as “Low risk” or “New customer”) calculated only from those counts. It never shows which shops, and never names or addresses.

Results come from courier records that sellers connect. They can be incomplete or out of date, and a number can change hands. A result is information to help you decide, not a statement about a person. The decision to ship, confirm or ask for an advance is yours.

Your courier records

When you connect a courier account or upload a file, you let us read your shipments (tracking number, customer mobile number, city, booking date and delivery status) and add them to the shared counts. You confirm you are allowed to share this data with us for that purpose.

Shared counts are totals: other sellers never see your shop name, your customers’ names or addresses, or which shop a parcel came from. You can disconnect a courier at any time; new shipments then stop coming in. When your account is closed and deleted, your records are removed from the shared counts.

Fair use

You agree not to:

  • check numbers that are not customers of your real orders;
  • check numbers in bulk or in sequence, copy results into another database, or resell or publish them;
  • use results to harass, shame or threaten anyone, or for decisions other than handling your own orders (for example credit, jobs or housing);
  • get around check limits, share your login outside the staff feature, or interfere with the service;
  • upload records you are not allowed to share, or false records.

We watch for misuse. Unusual patterns pause checks automatically while we review them, and we may suspend or close accounts that break these rules.

Customers can correct their history

Anyone can prove a number is theirs, see the same counts a shop sees, and ask for a correction. While a request is open, shops see “Under review” for that number. We decide within 14 days using courier evidence and may change the counts. Please answer our questions about a disputed parcel honestly.

Plans, credits and payments

Prices are shown in the app in Pakistani rupees. The free plan gives a set number of checks a day. Paid plans last 30 days; credit packs never expire and are used after your plan’s checks run out. Checking the same number again within 24 hours is free, and a check that fails does not count.

Bank and wallet transfers are confirmed after we see your receipt. Refunds are made by us where required by law or where we decide a refund is fair; a pack refund removes its unused credits and a plan refund ends the plan.

Business verification

We may ask for a CNIC or NTN to verify a business. We use it only for verification and to stop duplicate accounts; the images and the typed-in number are deleted 30 days after our decision.

Availability and liability

We work to keep RiskRadar available and accurate, but it is provided “as is”. If we can’t reach our servers, the app says so; it never shows an outage as “no history”.

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for decisions you make using results, or for indirect losses, and our total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in the 3 months before the claim.

Closing an account

The owner can close the account in the app at any time. Everyone is logged out at once and the account’s data is deleted after 30 days. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, and will tell you why unless the law or an investigation prevents it.

Changes, law and contact

If we change these terms, the app asks you to accept the new version before you continue. These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan, and the courts of Lahore have jurisdiction.

Questions: usmanjamil047@gmail.com or the support link in the app.
